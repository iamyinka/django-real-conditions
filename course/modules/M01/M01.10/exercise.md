# Exercise — M01.10 Shipping the skeleton

**Start from:** your fork, at `main` (which contains the Module 1 skeleton)

## Task
Ship one of your earlier Module 1 exercise solutions (M1.5, M1.8 or M1.9) to your fork's `main` through the full loop, with a review you write yourself and label honestly.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An issue with acceptance criteria exists, and the PR closes it.
- [ ] The branch has at least two commits, and every changed file is intentional: `git diff main...HEAD --stat` contains no scratch or local files.
- [ ] The PR body has a Verification section with the exact commands you ran and their results, and states which checks did **not** run.
- [ ] At least one review comment, marked `**Simulated review:**`, is answered with a **new commit** and a reply describing how you verified the fix, then resolved.
- [ ] After the squash merge, `git log --oneline -1 origin/main` ends in `(#<PR>)`, and `git diff --quiet <your-branch-tip> origin/main -- projects/telecom` exits 0.

## Hints
- Tag your branch tip before merging (`git tag exercise/m1.10-end`), so you can still compare after the branch is deleted.

## Solution
No solution tag: the acceptance criteria are checkable in your fork.
