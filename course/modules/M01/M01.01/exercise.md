# Exercise — M01.01 The telecom field-service problem

**Start from:** your fork, at `main`

## Task
Write the issue for a future feature: **customers can see the status of their ticket without logging in, using a link from their text message.** Don't build it; scope it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The issue has a title, a why, a scope, an out-of-scope list, and at least three acceptance criteria.
- [ ] Every acceptance criterion can be checked with a command, a test, or a specific browser action, with its expected result.
- [ ] The out-of-scope list names at least one tempting extra feature you're deliberately excluding.
- [ ] At least one criterion covers a failure: what happens with an expired or guessed link?

## Hints
- Think about who else could get the link, and what they'd see.

## Solution
A sample issue is in `solution/M01.01` (`git show solution/M01.01:course/solutions/M01.01-issue.md`, created before recording). Compare your criteria with it; there's more than one good answer.
